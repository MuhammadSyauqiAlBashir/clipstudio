Clip Studio is a private, non-commercial software tool used only by its operator (the creator behind bashclipeveryday) and one household member. It is not available for sign-up.

Use

  • Only videos the operator has permission to reuse are processed: their own content, footage provided by clipping campaigns, creators who explicitly allow clipping, or videos under a licence that allows reuse (e.g. Creative Commons Attribution). Movies, TV, studio and sports footage are refused, and segments with music are excluded.
  • Nothing is published automatically: every clip is reviewed and approved by the operator before it is uploaded to the operator's own accounts, with credit to the original creator.
  • Uploads follow each platform's own terms (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube) and rate limits.
