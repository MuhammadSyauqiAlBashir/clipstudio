Privacy Policy

bashclipeveryday (Clip Studio) · last updated 4 October 2026

Clip Studio is a private tool operated by an individual creator for the social media accounts bashclipeveryday. It turns long videos that the operator is allowed to reuse into short vertical clips, and — only after the operator approves a clip — posts it to the operator's own TikTok, Instagram and YouTube accounts. It is not offered to the public; only the operator (and one household member) can sign in.

What data the app uses

  • Platform account access. When the operator connects their own TikTok, Instagram or YouTube account, the app receives an access token and basic account information (account ID, username, display name, avatar). These are used only to upload the operator's approved clips to that same account and to show which account is connected.
  • Videos and captions. Source videos the operator adds, the clips made from them, their transcripts and captions.
  • Sign-in. The operator's username for this app and a session cookie.

The app does not collect data about other TikTok, Instagram or YouTube users, does not read followers, messages or comments, does not use data for advertising, and never sells or shares data with anyone.

Where it is stored and for how long

All data is stored on the operator's own server in Indonesia. Access tokens are stored only on that server, readable only by the app's own system account, and are deleted when the account is disconnected. Source videos are deleted after the clips are decided (at most 7 days); clips are deleted 30 days after they are posted or rejected.

Your choices

A connected account can be disconnected at any time in the app, or by removing the app in the platform's own settings (TikTok: Settings → Security → Manage app permissions; Instagram: Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites; Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions). To ask for deletion of any data, email the contact below.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube uploads are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
